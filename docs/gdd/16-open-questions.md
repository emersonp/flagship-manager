7. **1h before or after legacy mode**
1h is designed next (section 15, item 53). Legacy mode (1f part two) tunes whole legacies, and tuning done against today's skills, attributes, and gear would mostly be redone once 1h changes them.
(a) Build 1h, after 1h-seams, before 1f part two (b) Keep the order 1f part two, 1g, 1h, and accept retuning after 1h
Recommend (a): legacy mode then tunes the mechanics the playtest keeps.

8. **Lines that read a person's condition**
A line's criteria read a person's caution, discipline, seniority, and whether they lead, but not their traits, morale, or injuries. Since slice 1f-previews the previews run on test ships, whose condition changes who goes and how the dice fall, but not which lines may hold, so a line cannot speak to a grieving lead or a hurt actor.
(a) Add a person's traits, morale, and injury as line facts (b) Leave lines to the mission's facts, and let storylets speak to condition
Recommend (a): designed with 1h, which reshapes what a character is.
