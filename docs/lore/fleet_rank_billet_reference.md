# Starfleet Analog: Unified 16-Grade Rank & Billet Rubric

## 1. Entry Points & Progression Rules
* **Single-Ladder System:** There is no social or legal divide between "enlisted" and "officer" classes. Every crew member climbs the same 16-grade progression.
* **Direct Enlistment (Grade 1):** Standard entry for recruits without higher degrees. Personnel advance through time-in-grade, practical certifications, and field evaluations.
* **Fleet Academy / OCS Graduates (Grade 5):** Completing the 4-year Fleet Academy or Officer Candidate School commissions recruits directly at Grade 5.
* **Accredited MDs & PhDs (Grade 6):** Medical doctors and research doctorates enter directly at Grade 6 to exercise immediate clinical or laboratory authority without requiring command-track prerequisites.
* **Staggered Unification:**
  * **Wave 1 (Grade 5):** Conn and Operations unify into **Ensign**; Tactical: Security and Tactical: Gunnery & Assault unify into **Tactical Ensign** (or *Second Lieutenant* in Marine field units).
  * **Wave 2 (Grade 7):** Tactical, Engineering, and Diplomatic unify into **Lieutenant**.
  * **Wave 3 (Grade 9):** Science: Research and Science: Medical unify with the rest of the fleet into **Commander**.

## 3. Hacker / Cyber-Warfare Placement Guide
* **Offensive Cyber-Warfare & Field Breaching:** **Tactical: Gunnery & Assault** (Electronic Warfare Corporal/Sergeant → Gunnery Lieutenant).
* **Counter-Intrusion, Network Defense & Digital Forensics:** **Tactical: Security** (Inspector/Warden → Provost).
* **Signals Intelligence (SIGINT), Encryption & Spoofing:** **Operations** (Comms Tech/Operations Specialist → Lieutenant).
* **AI/VI Architecture & Quantum Cryptanalysis:** **Science: Research** (Fellow → Principal Investigator).

## 4. Major Shipboard Billets (Job Titles vs. Rank)

| Billet Title | Division | Typical Grade Range | Primary Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Commanding Officer (CO)** | Unified Command | Grades 9–12 | Ultimate command authority aboard a vessel or station. |
| **Executive Officer (XO)** | Unified Command | Grades 8–9 | Second-in-command; manages daily ship operations, crew, and department heads. |
| **Chief of the Boat (COB)** | Any *(Non-Command)* | Grades 7–8 | Senior specialist ombudsman advising the CO/XO on crew morale and readiness. |
| **Chief Tactical Officer (CTO)** | Tactical | Grades 7–8 | Runs ship weapons, shields, point-defense, and electronic warfare. |
| **Chief of Security** | Tactical: Security | Grades 6–7 | Enforces shipboard law, manages the brig, and oversees internal security. |
| **Marine Detachment Commander** | Tactical: Assault | Grades 6–7 | Commands embarked Marines for boarding actions and planetary drops. |
| **Armorer** | Tactical: Assault | Grades 3–4 | Manages the ship's armory, ordnance, and hardsuit readiness. |
| **Chief Pilot / Chief Helmsman** | Conn | Grades 4–7 | Primary pilot during combat maneuvers and orbital insertions. |
| **Astrogator** | Conn | Grades 6–7 | Plots FTL jump vectors and tracks spatial anomalies. |
| **Chief Engineer** | Engineering | Grades 6–8 | Oversees the reactor, FTL drive, hull integrity, and damage control. |
| **Damage Control Officer (DCO)** | Engineering | Grades 5–6 | Coordinates emergency bulkhead seals, firefighting, and hull repairs in battle. |
| **Chief Operations Officer** | Operations | Grades 7–8 | Manages ship power distribution, sensor bandwidth, and shuttle/transporter logistics. |
| **Quartermaster** | Operations | Grades 3–6 | Controls cargo bays, spare parts, consumables, and requisition manifests. |
| **Chief Science Officer (CSO)** | Science: Research | Grades 8–9 | Leads planetary surveys, xenobiology/astrophysics labs, and deep-sensor analysis. |
| **Chief Medical Officer (CMO)** | Science: Medical | Grades 6–8 | Runs sickbay; holds legal authority to quarantine the ship or relieve the CO on medical grounds. |
| **Chief Diplomatic Officer** | Diplomatic | Grades 7–8 | Advises the CO on treaties, interstellar law, and first-contact protocol. |
