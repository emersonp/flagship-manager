# Calendar

### Which calendars?
The default calendar of the game is the Bastion Calendar. There will be other calendars, which will have a translation / projection formula.

## The Bastion Calendar

### Years
Years are made up of 12 months. The game starts in the year 105.

### Months
Months are made up of 5 weeks and are exactly 30 days.

- Krellis _(KREL-iss)_
- Vortun _(VOR-toon)_
- Zhortuna _(ZHOR-too-nah)_
- Moss _(MAWSS)_
- Pharoska _(FAIR-osk-ah)_
- Pelpheär _(PEL-fee-ahr)_
- Barx _(BARKS)_
- Bast _(BASST)_
- Venjenten _(ven-JEN-ten)_
- Almonten _(al-MOAN-ten)_
- Orphelten _(or-FEL-ten)_
- Othra _(AWTH-rah)_

*Note to future self: The Bastion Calendar is just the Imperial Calendar with a new era, starting 105 years ago.*

### Weeks 
Weeks are 6 days.

- Stoneday / Stone
- Quorday / Quor
- Kinclause
- Embertally / Ember
- Menday
- Hearthfall / Hearth

*Note to future self: Bastion days of the week are pulled from the Conclave, dropping two days from their eight day week: Boughday and Waudit.
